ClientSnap has closed. Thank you.

ClientSnap shut down on November 6, 2026, after almost a year as a free beta.

Thank you to every trainer who tried it, logged sessions with it, and sent feedback. Building a simple tool for personal trainers was a joy, but not enough people used it regularly to keep it running.

The app is no longer available, and new accounts can't be created.
